# My Homelessness Journey ... July–September 2026

*A first-person record of shelter life, finding work, gaining housing, and the people I met along the way in Chico, California.*

I began sharing my experiences publicly in July 2026, while I was staying at the Torres Shelter. I wasn't looking for sympathy. I wanted people to understand what it feels like to enter the homelessness system, what it takes to navigate it, and how people can become invisible once they're part of it.

Some of these chapters are lightly edited Facebook posts written **as events happened**. Others are clearly marked later reflections, written after I found housing. The dates below follow the events and original posts, not necessarily the dates the articles were added to this site.

## About these accounts

These are **personal accounts**, not official descriptions of shelter policies or social-service eligibility. When I describe rules, conditions, or what people told me, I am sharing my understanding at the time. I may have been wrong about some details, and policies can change. Where later context is added, I try to label it rather than silently rewrite history.

The original photos reproduced in some chapters came from my Facebook archive. No photos of other shelter residents are included.
