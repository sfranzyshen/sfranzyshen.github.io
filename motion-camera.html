Local Motion Camera

Your webcam feed stays on this computer. When armed, motion saves one or two JPG images into a Motion Camera folder it creates for you.

Motion level: 0.0%

1. Start the camera. 2. Choose a save folder. 3. Arm detection.

Keep this page open and the computer awake. Choose Pictures when asked. The app will create and use Pictures\Motion Camera, avoiding a current Chrome subfolder-picker bug.

This file can be opened directly from your computer in Chrome or Edge. It needs camera permission and a browser that supports choosing a local folder. GitHub Pages also works because it is served over HTTPS.
