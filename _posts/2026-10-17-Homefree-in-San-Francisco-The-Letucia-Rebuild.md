*This was my home for a little while ... 😁*

## A home on wheels

I lived in this van for the first half of a full rebuild of **Letucia**, a Burning Man art car we were working on over on **Treasure Island**.

I was living in **San Francisco, around Golden Gate and Webster**, during that period. Later, I spent time at the **North Beach Hotel**, going back and forth between staying there and living in the van while the rebuild continued.

It wasn't a conventional way of living ... but I loved that time in my life.

## The city and the people

I loved North Beach. I loved being able to spend time in that part of San Francisco ... but really, I loved **all of the city**.

The work on Letucia was part of the adventure, but so were the experiences, the people, and the friendships that came out of those days.

There are stories from that period that could fill volumes of a book someday. And some of the friendships I made will last a lifetime.

**I wouldn't trade that chapter of my life for any amount of money.**

If I could find a way to live an alternative life in San Francisco again, I would.

## Homeless ... or homefree?

Yes, I was technically homeless during part of that time.

But back then, I preferred to call it **“homefree.”** 🙂

I had a home ... it just happened to have wheels.

That word captured how I felt about the life I was living then. It was an adventure I remember with affection, and one I chose to embrace.

My later experiences navigating homelessness in Chico have been very different. Both are part of my story, and I don't want to pretend that every experience of living without conventional housing feels the same.

When I look at this photograph, I don't just see an old van.

I see **a chapter of my life that I loved** ... a city I still love ... and friendships that no amount of money could replace.
