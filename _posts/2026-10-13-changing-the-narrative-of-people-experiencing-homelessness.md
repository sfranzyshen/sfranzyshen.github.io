date: 2026-10-13

Homelessness is a housing problem ... not a character flaw.

People experiencing homelessness are our neighbors. They are individuals with names, histories, talents, struggles, and hopes. Too often, public conversations focus on encampments and visibility instead of people and the housing they need.

Changing the narrative can begin with something simple: make eye contact. Say good morning. Say hello, neighbor. Acknowledge another human being.

Respect and dignity won't solve the housing crisis by themselves ... but they are where a humane response begins.

*This is a short test post as I work on sharing more stories and ideas.*
