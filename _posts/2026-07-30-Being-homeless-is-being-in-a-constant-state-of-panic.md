Being homeless is being in a constant state of panic ... Panic attacks becomes the norm... 

Oh my God where's my phone... Where's my keys... several times a day... If you're lucky enough to have a place to safely store belongings... otherwise you are stuck carrying everything around with you everywhere ... Your at the mercy of thieves, opportunist and your own forgetfulness.... And if you do partake in the finer things in life ... You can't enter the shelter with any paraphernalia or substances ... So you have to find a place to stash your stash... There's a lot that goes into stashing ... The Number one thing to keep in mind that a kid could find it ... So no playgrounds, parks, creek beds or housing areas... You also have to be careful not to let anyone else know what you're up to ... or risk your stash being taken...
Today I received word from the Jesus Center Renewal Senior Shelter that I have an intake at 1:30p tomorrow... This is hopefully a 👍 step forward for me ... Hopefully I'll have a safer place to keep my belongings so I don't need to carry a bag around....
Peace to everyone
