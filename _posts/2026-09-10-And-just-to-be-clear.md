And just to be clear ... this isn't a plea for help. It's just another chapter in my ongoing adventures in US America. 😁

Apparently, the adventure continues ...
After going from an unemployed homeless individual to working part-time and taking rent on a private room ... my food assistance has been cut from $9.93 per day to just $2.90 per day.
In this current environment, I was already having problems with having enough each month ... before making these changes.
I was under the impression that I would be able to earn up to my IRT without it affecting my benefits ... but this is obviously not the case.
I figured that there would be some sort of change ... but not a 70% reduction. That was a surprise!
I am only working 16 hours per week ... and nearly half my income goes directly toward rent and utilities.
This is just another example of a system that can be (and is) punitive at times ... intentional or not ... when it is needed the most.
Right now, my biggest fear ... is that becoming employed and housed will disqualify me from my Medi-Cal assistance ... that I am depending on for my life! Literally. 😁
