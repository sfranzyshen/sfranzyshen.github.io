Now for the reality of things … I am actually doing great!

When compared to some of the people who have fallen into the cracks of this society and are forever locked into a system that never quite helps them reach a stable environment for their own situation, I am doing great! While I am experiencing and sharing my homeless journey … please know I will be fine … I will make it … I will once again stand on my own two feet … so don't feel sorry for me ... feel sorry for the many of our brothers and sisters who are in the system of poverty and are hopelessly trapped ... because while I may be temporarily staying at the shelter ... I am not going to be for long. 😁
