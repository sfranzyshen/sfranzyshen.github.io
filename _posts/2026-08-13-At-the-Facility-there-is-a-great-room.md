At the "Facility" there is a great room with two wings ... and each wing has two halls ... each hall has seven rooms ... with two people per room ... that works out to be fifty six people who are under the care of this place ... I see it is very much a business around here ... and the cracks of capitalism shines brightly even in the nonprofit sectors in the USA ... 

Please don't take anything I say as criticism of this place or especially the people ... all of my interactions with the staff have been nothing but polite and respectful ... the facility is as clean as any hospital type environment ... although I wouldn't want to have to recover from surgery here ... I also wonder if they are really here to help the individual ... or just help treat them ... It's a numbers game ... You get six months here before you're kicked out ... My roommate recently timed out ... and the staff handled it politely but firmly ... It also seems they slow rolled the process to keep you here ... until your time is up? The six months thing isn't a tough love reason ... It's because the insurance companies won't pay any more ... not that you don't still have the need ... But now they can't afford to help you ... I really don't think anyone actually gets housing through this program ... and if they do it's not because they became employed ... It's because their medical needs paid for it ...
