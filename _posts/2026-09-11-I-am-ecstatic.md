**I am ecstatic!** I contacted the social services concerned about them cutting my medical assistance like they did my food assistance ... they assured me that I will not lose my health insurance …

## A correction I almost missed

The worker then proceeded to also see that they didn't apply the self-employment standard deduction offset to the CalFresh equation and that I should be getting more in food assistance and not less ... and that my IRT (reporting threshold) has also gone up! I'll now be getting $299 per month for food assistance and a new IRT of $1,729 🙂 
## What if I hadn't called?

Maybe the system isn't completely broken ... but it sure feels fishy that the errors always fall in their favor ... until you call them on it 🤔 and what if I never said anything? ... what if I wasn't capable of speaking up for myself? I'd be struggling to figure out how to make $87 work every month ... like so many other poor people out there that are dealing with this bureaucracy …
