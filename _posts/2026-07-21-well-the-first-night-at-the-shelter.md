... well the first night at the shelter was utterly awful ... the entire place smells like dirty feet, even the cafeteria! I was only there for 5 minutes before the first lockdown happened. 

Everyone was forced into the dorms, and we sat on our bunks for an hour while the police and EMS intervened in a fight between two men  ... as the staff cleaned up the blood ... after lights out even with noise-canceling headphones, 
I can still hear the talking, coughing, and snores of other people ... but I managed to get some sleep ... only to be awoken again at 4 a.m. ... at least this time it didn't involve the police ... 
I had to wait until 5 a.m. before I was allowed to leave ... being locked in a building for 11 hours a day is not going to work well for me for very long …
