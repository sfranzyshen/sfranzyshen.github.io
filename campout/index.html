Chico Campout for Compassion 2026

Saturday, November 14, 2026

One Night. Many Locations. One Community.

A communitywide night of awareness, understanding, and compassion for our neighbors experiencing homelessness.

You don't have to camp out to participate!

Join a discussion, invite a speaker, host an activity, support a basic-needs drive, or simply share the event with others. An overnight campout is optional.

Why We're Here ... A Conversation, Not an Argument

Our approach to homelessness may appear left-leaning ... but this isn't about left or right. Facts are facts, and results are results. The best available research should help us understand what works ... not determine which side wins an argument.

We're not here to tell anyone what to think, or how it should be done. We welcome countering material and different perspectives. We want a dialogue ... nothing more.

What is Campout for Compassion?

For one shared night, Chico residents, families, schools, churches, clubs, and other organizations are invited to take part in whatever way works for them. Some may hold supervised overnight campouts at appropriate locations, but camping is not required.

Each group plans its own gathering. The evening might include a speaker with firsthand experience of homelessness, respectful questions and discussion, age-appropriate learning activities, reflection, or an optional collection of requested food and basic necessities for an existing local program.

This is an awareness event, not a fundraiser. There is no central campaign fund, required donation, or participation fee. Groups that choose to collect goods or money can give directly to an existing local organization of their choosing.

One night outside cannot recreate homelessness. It can, however, begin a conversation about daily realities that many of our neighbors face ... and how we treat one another.

How your group can participate

1. Choose how to participate

Join a conversation, invite a speaker, organize an activity, collect basic necessities, share information ... or host an optional campout.

2. Plan your activity

Choose a safe, accessible format. Overnight hosts should arrange permission, bathrooms, supervision and weather alternatives.

3. Learn and listen

Invite a guest speaker, have a respectful conversation, use a learning activity, or reflect together on housing and community.

4. Connect with Chico

Let the organizers know you're participating, and request a public map pin if your group wants one.

You don't have to camp out to participate. An evening discussion, classroom activity, basic-needs drive, volunteer gathering, or simply sharing the invitation all contribute to the same communitywide awareness effort.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everyone is welcome ... and you do not have to camp out to participate. Here are answers to some of the questions people may have about November 14.

1. Do I have to camp out overnight to participate?

Absolutely not! You can join or host a conversation, invite a speaker, organize an educational activity, share information, or help spread the word. An overnight campout is completely optional.

2. Can I participate from my own home?

Yes! Individuals and families can participate at home without camping. Watch an educational video, have a conversation with friends or family, reflect on what you learn, or share information with your neighbors.

3. Can our church participate without hosting a campout?

Absolutely! Churches and other faith communities can share the invitation with their congregations, include it in a bulletin or announcement, host an optional discussion, invite a speaker, or encourage members to participate individually or at home. Your congregation does not need to organize its own campout.

4. Are we pretending to experience homelessness?

No. Spending one night outside cannot reproduce the realities of homelessness. Our purpose is not to simulate homelessness ... it is to encourage awareness, compassion, respectful listening, and meaningful conversations with our neighbors.

5. What is the purpose of Chico Campout for Compassion?

One Night. Many Locations. One Community. We want to bring Chico residents together around a shared evening of learning and conversation about homelessness, with room for different experiences and points of view.

6. When and where is the event happening?

Saturday, November 14, 2026. Participants may take part at home or in gatherings organized by churches, schools, community groups, and other hosts across Chico. This is not a single centralized campsite. Any publicly listed participating locations will appear in the locations section with the host's permission.

7. Do I need permission to organize an overnight campout?

Any overnight host should obtain the property owner's permission and check with the City of Chico or other relevant authority about applicable requirements before making plans. Hosts are responsible for appropriate restrooms, safety, supervision, accessibility, and a weather alternative. We have asked the City for clarification; do not assume that an overnight activity has already been approved. You can also participate without camping.

8. Can children, youth groups, and families participate?

Yes. Choose age-appropriate activities and make sure responsible adults supervise minors. Families may participate at home, and groups should follow their own youth-safety policies and any applicable location requirements.

9. Is this a fundraising campaign?

No. There is no central campaign fund, fee, or required donation. Groups that independently choose to collect requested food or basic necessities can give directly to an existing local organization of their choosing.

10. Do I have to agree with a particular political position or approach to homelessness?

No. You do not have to agree on a particular policy or use our recommended resources to take part. We hope people with different perspectives will find common ground, listen to one another, and have respectful conversations.

11. What kinds of educational activities can we organize?

Ideas include a small-group discussion, documentary or video screening, guest speaker with lived experience, classroom or youth activity, community reflection, or sharing practical local information. See our free resources and activity guides for ideas.

12. How do I get involved or contact the organizer?

Participate in a way that works for you, invite others, or contact Scotty Franzyshen at scotty@sfranzyshen.org. We'd love to hear whether you or your group might participate.

Still have questions? Email us ... we'll update these answers as plans develop and new questions come in.

Shared Values, Shared Purpose ... Our Starting Point

Before we debate strategies, what values do we share? That is the challenge in The Missing Piece in Ending Homelessness: Shared Values by Iain De Jong of OrgCode ... the video we are using as a core conversation starter for Campout for Compassion.

We do not have to agree on every policy to begin with shared humanity. Our aim is to listen, examine the values behind our choices, include people with lived experience in meaningful ways, and ask whether our responses move neighbors toward safe, stable housing.

Participation is open even if your group does not use our educational resources. These resources are recommended, not required; our selection reflects a shelter-first and lowest-barrier approach, and we do not recommend treatment-first materials.

Recommended Resources ... Not Required

Our educational materials reflect a “shelter first” and lowest-barrier approach to addressing homelessness. They are recommended resources, not required material. If your group chooses not to use them, your participation is still encouraged.

Awareness doesn't take sides ... all voices and perspectives are encouraged. However, Campout for Compassion will not provide or recommend “treatment first” materials.
