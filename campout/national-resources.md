# November 2026 ... Complete National Resource & Reference List

This is the source register for the **November 2026 addition** to Chico Campout for Compassion. It preserves the national and state references, every unique national PDF submitted for review, their intended uses, and caveats. Resources are **recommended, not required**. These are **external source materials**, not endorsements of our independent local event.

## A. National and California observances

1. **[California Homeless Education Technical Assistance Center (HETAC): National Homeless Youth Awareness Month toolkit](https://www.hetac.org/hetac-resources/youth-awareness)** ... background on the 2007 congressional recognition; sample school-board resolution, school calendar and activities, press release, books and films, educational videos, social posts, and optional November 10 #WearGreenDay.
2. **[California Senate Concurrent Resolution 197 (2026)](https://legiscan.com/CA/text/SCR197/id/3457347)** ... designation of **November 2026** as California Youth Homelessness Outreach, Prevention, and Education (HOPE) Month.
3. **[Youth HOPE Month ... National Runaway Safeline](https://www.1800runaway.org/)** ... additional youth prevention and awareness context; the HETAC toolkit links to its dedicated Youth HOPE materials.
4. **[Hunger and Homelessness Awareness Week ... official campaign](https://hhweek.org/)** ... **November 15–21, 2026** per the campaign homepage; annual nationwide education, service, and action effort.
5. **[Hunger and homelessness ... HHWeek background and actions](https://hhweek.org/hunger-and-homelessness/)** ... context and activity ideas. **Some headline figures on this page are dated and should not be repeated as 2026 statistics.**

## B. Original HHWeek toolkit PDFs supplied for review

These entries preserve **all thirteen unique PDF titles** supplied in the request. The lobbying PDF was submitted twice and is listed only once. Some older documents use dated language and activities we do **not** recommend for our particular Campout.

1. **[Hunger and Homelessness Awareness Week Toolkit (2024 PDF)](https://hhweek.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/Hunger-and-Homelessness-Awareness-Week-Toolkit.pdf)** ... model for resource sharing, youth/student education, consented testimonials, public awareness, and community action. **Many included data points are historical.**
2. **[Hunger and Homelessness Awareness Week Campus Organizing Toolkit (2024 PDF)](https://hhweek.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/H_HWeek-Campus-Organizing-Toolkit.pdf)** ... event-planning roles, community collaboration, outreach, publicity, recipient coordination, and quality-over-quantity planning. Parts were designed for campuses and pandemic-era conditions.
3. **[Dining Hall Fast (PDF)](https://hhweek.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/Dining_Hall_Fast.pdf)** ... campus dining-service concept; **context only, not a Campout recommended activity**, especially where meal skipping may be harmful or exclusionary.
4. **[Faces of Homelessness Panel (PDF)](https://hhweek.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/Faces_of_Homelessness_Panel.pdf)** ... inviting people with lived experience into a dialogue; adapt with informed consent, participant control, transportation, appropriate compensation, and no invasive questions.
5. **[Homeless Challenge Project (PDF)](https://hhweek.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/Homeless_Challenge_Project.pdf)** ... historical street-immersion exercise; **not adopted or recommended** because staged deprivation, impersonation, safety risks, and use of already-stretched services conflict with the Campout's safer educational model.
6. **[Lobbying Elected Officials (PDF)](https://hhweek.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/Lobbying_Elected_Officials.pdf)** ... preparing two or three well-researched topics and respectful civic meetings; the Campout can invite evidence-based questions without requiring political agreement.
7. **[One Night Without a Home (PDF)](https://hhweek.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/One_Night_Without_a_Home.pdf)** ... a historical model for the optional reflection-and-discussion sleep-out; **our Nov. 14 event is separately organized, distributed across permitted sites, and does not claim to simulate homelessness**.
8. **[Outreach Run (PDF)](https://hhweek.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/Outreach_Run.pdf)** ... listening, coordination with existing outreach, and distributing requested goods; adapt with consent, dignity, advance recipient coordination, and no uninvited or unsafe visits.
9. **[Hunger Banquet (PDF)](https://hhweek.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/Hunger_Banquet.pdf)** ... inequality/food access teaching model; **not a default recommendation**. Consider its educational questions without imposing unequal meals or hunger on participants.
10. **[Walk a Mile in My Shoes (PDF)](https://hhweek.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/Walk_a_Mile_in_My_Shoes.pdf)** ... historical peer-learning concept; prefer voluntarily invited conversation over shadowing, intrusive visits, or portraying one person's experience as a simulation.
11. **[Sample Press Release (PDF)](https://hhweek.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/Sample_Press_Release.pdf)** ... format guidance for an original Chico release; do not claim an affiliation, speaker, event, or donor not confirmed locally.
12. **[Sample Proclamation (PDF)](https://hhweek.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/Sample_Proclamation.pdf)** ... a model for local governments and school boards to recognize hunger/homelessness awareness; **sample only, not an actual Chico proclamation**.
13. **[Sample Schedule of Events (PDF)](https://hhweek.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/Sample_Schedule_of_Events.pdf)** ... supplied as a reference. **The linked PDF could not be retrieved during this review**, so its contents are not represented as reviewed or verified.

## Source-use and attribution policy

**Verified observation dates:** Source links checked October 31, 2026. The current awareness dates come from the HETAC, California legislative, and HHWeek pages. The NCH event listing elsewhere uses a **broader November 14–22 window**, while the HHWeek main campaign advertises **November 15–21**; we use the latter for our public calendar. Links, calendars, and donation needs may change.

**Statistics:** Sources include older national figures from 2016–2022. Do **not** carry them into 2026 flyers without updating the source, reference year, geography, and definition. A point-in-time homelessness count is not an annual count of everyone who experiences homelessness.

**Copyright:** We link to original national toolkits and describe their ideas; we do not republish entire texts or imply they were authored by Chico organizers. The Campus Organizing Toolkit contains a CC BY 4.0 attribution note; source links and credit are preserved.

**Editorial commitments:** Person-first wording; informed consent for stories and images; age-appropriate learning; safety and weather planning; no deprivation simulations; no compulsory donations; no promise of housing or services; housing affordability and stability remain central to the Campout discussion.

**Project identity:** Chico Campout for Compassion is independently organized. Linking national organizations does not mean sponsorship, endorsement, registration, or partnership.
