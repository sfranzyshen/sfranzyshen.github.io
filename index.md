# Navigating America from the Bottom Up

### A Quick Disclaimer:

I am not a housing professional, lawyer, policy expert, social worker, economist, or government official. What you are reading here is my own personal interpretation, research, observations, opinions, questions, frustrations, and occasional know-it-all blowhardness about homelessness, housing, poverty, bureaucracy, and the systems surrounding them. I try to verify facts and link to source material whenever possible, but I can be wrong, misunderstand something, miss context, or reach conclusions others disagree with.

### This site is self-expression, not professional advice.

I am writing from my own experiences, what I have witnessed, what I have researched, and what I think deserves to be questioned. Read it. Question it. Check my sources. Correct me when I’m wrong.  The point is not that I have all the answers. The point is that I think we should be asking a lot more questions.
I am opinionated and blunt in my ways ... but I try not to be critical or judgmental in my actions. I consider myself a Human Rights Enforcer, Non-Prophet, Community Support Specialist and I expect nothing in return for my kindness. 

#### Either help me to do it ... or get out of my way from doing it ... because either way we are changing the world!

#### If I can be useful, I’m here.

#### If you need my help, reach out.

#### If I can’t help … maybe I know who can.
